title: "Image Generation"

---

Visuals make your app stand out. The **Media** tab lets you generate custom photos, illustrations, icons, and backgrounds without leaving Vibecode, then turn any image into a short video. Describe what you want, then add the result to your prompt so the agent puts it in your app.

## Generate an Image

1. In the web app, open the **Media** tab in your project
2. Make sure **Image** is selected
3. Pick a **Model**: **GPT Image 2** (the default), **Nano Banana 2**, or **Nano Banana 2 Lite**
4. Optionally pick a **Type**, such as **Icon**, **Character**, **Background**, or **Component**, and a **Style**
5. Describe the image to generate and send

<Frame caption="The Media tab: Image and Video modes, model, type, and style">
  <img src="/images/screens/media-tab.png" alt="The Media tab: Image and Video modes, model, type, and style" />
</Frame>

Your images appear in the Media tab gallery. Open one to regenerate it, use it as a reference for a new image, or click **Add to chat** to add it to your prompt.

<Tip>Choose the **Icon** type when you want an app icon or an in-app icon.</Tip>

## Use Images in Your App

Click **Add to chat** on an image, then tell the agent where it goes:

```
Put this image at the top of the welcome screen, above the sign-up button.
```

To replace an image, generate a new one, add it to chat, and say which image it replaces.

## Animate an Image Into Video

1. Open an image and click **Animate**, or switch the Media tab to **Video** and pick an image as the reference
2. Describe the motion to animate
3. Pick the **Duration**, **Resolution**, and **Aspect**, then send

Generated videos show up in the same gallery, and you can add them to chat the same way.

<Note>Generating images and videos uses credits. See [Plans and Credits](/getting-started/plans-and-credits).</Note>
