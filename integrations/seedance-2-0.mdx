Seedance 2.0 is the cheaper, faster ByteDance video model. Clips run up to 15 seconds and it can render all the way to 4K.

It supports text-to-video and image-to-video with generated audio.

## Best For

- Short video clips
- Social content
- Animating a still image
- High-resolution video

## Features

- Text-to-video and image-to-video
- Clips up to 15 seconds
- Up to 4K
- Generated audio

## Ask Vibecode

Tell the AI:

> "Add Seedance 2.0 to my app so users can create short videos from a prompt or photo."

The AI will set up the integration automatically.

## Choose This If

Choose Seedance 2.0 for shorter clips at a lower cost. For longer, more coherent shots, use [Seedance 2.5](/integrations/seedance-2-5).
