Grok 4.6 is an xAI model that is great at coding and agentic tasks, and it is much more fun to talk to.

Grok is generally less scripted and will speak its mind more freely. It's also trained on a lot of Twitter data, so it has a different personality than the rest of the AI chatbots.

## Best For

- Coding help
- Social and creative content
- Entertainment apps

## Features

- Unique personality
- Less scripted responses
- Good for engaging content
