---

Channel3 is an API for products. Search almost any product online by text, image, or URL across thousands of brands and retailers.

Results come back with clean images, current prices, availability, and buy links, with filters for price, brand, and category.

## Best For

- Shopping apps
- Gift finders
- Price comparison tools
- AI shopping assistants

## Features

- Search by text, image, or product URL
- Current prices and availability
- Buy links
- Filters for price, brand, and category

## Ask Vibecode

Tell the AI:

> "Add Channel3 product search to my app so users can find products and compare prices."

The AI will set up the integration automatically.

## Choose This If

Choose Channel3 when your app needs real product data. For other data sources, see [TMDB](/integrations/tmdb) or [OpenWeather](/integrations/openweather-api).
