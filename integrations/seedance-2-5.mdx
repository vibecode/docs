Seedance 2.5 is ByteDance's newest video model. It reasons about the whole shot at once, so motion, lighting, and subject identity stay coherent from the first frame to the last.

It supports text-to-video and image-to-video with generated audio, up to 30 seconds in a single shot.

## Best For

- Longer video clips
- Cinematic shots
- Animating a still image
- Videos with sound

## Features

- Text-to-video and image-to-video
- Clips up to 30 seconds
- Generated audio
- Up to 1080p

## Ask Vibecode

Tell the AI:

> "Add Seedance 2.5 to my app so users can turn a prompt or photo into a short video with sound."

The AI will set up the integration automatically.

## Choose This If

Choose Seedance 2.5 for longer, coherent clips. It is expensive, so keep clips short while testing. For a cheaper, faster option, use [Seedance 2.0](/integrations/seedance-2-0).
