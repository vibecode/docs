<Update label="September 10th, 2026" tags={Launch} description="Native iOS apps">
  - Native Swift apps are available to everyone. Pick **iOS app** when you create a project to build a SwiftUI app for iPhone and iPad.
  - Apps run in a cloud iOS Simulator streamed live to your browser or phone, so you don't need a Mac or Xcode
  - Install your app on your own iPhone, or publish it to the App Store, directly from Vibecode
  - Read the [Native iOS Apps guide](/getting-started/native-ios-apps)
</Update>

<Update label="June 17th, 2025" tags={Launch} description="App Store">

  - Reached #2 on the US App Store for Developer Tools, and top 10 in 50+ countries on launch day
