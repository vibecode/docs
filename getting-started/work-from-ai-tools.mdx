<Note>The Vibecode MCP replaces SSH access. SSH connections to Vibecode projects are no longer available, so use the MCP to work on your code from your own tools.</Note>

To work on your Vibecode project from your own computer, connect the [Vibecode MCP](/guides/vibecode-mcp) to Claude Code, Cursor, Codex or another AI coding tool. Your agent can read your project, make changes and follow your builds, and the changes show up in your Vibecode preview.

Every plan can connect the MCP, including Free. Pro and Max get full access, including terminal commands in your workspace, your live server and the App Store tools.
