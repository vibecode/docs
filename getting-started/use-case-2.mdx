## Getting started

Remixing is fastest if your account has credits. Copying Pixgram is free, but running it, building from a prompt and each change use credits, which come with [paid plans](/getting-started/plans-and-credits) from $20 a month. New accounts that sign up with Google or Apple get their first app build free, but only on their first project. If you're new, [start from a prompt](#start-from-a-prompt) before you remix anything.

### Remix the template

1. Open the [Instagram clone template](https://www.vibecodeapp.com/templates/instagram-clone) and click **Remix this template**.
2. Click **Remix Pixgram**, or **Remix this app** on a phone. If you're new, you answer a few questions and sign up, and Vibecode makes your copy. You can skip the plan offer that follows with **Continue to my app**.
3. Your copy opens in the workspace. With credits on your account, it runs in the cloud iOS simulator. In our paid-account test on October 20, 2026, it was running a minute after the click.
4. Ask for changes in chat, like "Rename the app to Crumb and make it a feed for home bakers."

### Start from a prompt

1. Signed out? Paste the [prompt below](#prompt-example) into the box on the [Vibecode homepage](https://www.vibecodeapp.com/), with **iPhone app** selected, and click **Build it**.
2. Signed in? Click **New app**, choose **iOS app** and send the prompt. In the iPhone app, tap **New project**, then **iOS app**.
3. Claude Code builds the app, tests it in the simulator and fixes what it finds. Our test build took 38 minutes on a paid account with Claude Opus 5.5.

## What you get

Pixgram comes with:

- A home feed with photo and carousel posts
- Stories with progress bars
- Double-tap likes, comments and bookmarks
- Explore with account search
- A Create flow for new posts
- Activity, profiles and direct messages

It doesn't have Reels, sign-up, photo uploads or push notifications yet. Ask the agent for each one.

## Why backend features matter

Pixgram runs on sample data, so it needs no backend to run. Real users need one:

- **Authentication.** People create accounts and sign in.
- **Database.** Your app saves posts, likes, comments and follows in the cloud.
- **Storage.** Your app keeps the photos people upload in the cloud.

To add them, open the **Database** tab in your project and click **Enable database**. Vibecode puts a setup prompt in the chat box. Send it, and the agent sets up sign-in and a database with Vibecode Cloud. Then ask for photo uploads, like "Let people post photos from their library." Vibecode Cloud is included from the Plus plan, and [Backend and Auth](/features/backend-auth) explains how it works.

## Prompt example

This is a 134-word version of the 785-word prompt that built Pixgram, which you can copy from the [Instagram clone guide](https://www.vibecodeapp.com/clones/instagram-clone#prompt). We sent it to a new iOS app on October 20, 2026, from a paid account with Claude Opus 5.5.

```text First prompt wrap
Build a native iPhone photo-sharing app called Pinhole, inspired by Instagram but with its own name, logo and colors. Use five tabs: Home, Explore, Create, Activity and Profile. Home has a stories row and a feed of photo posts. Double-tap a photo to like it with a heart animation, and open comments in a bottom sheet. Stories open full screen with progress bars. Explore is a photo grid with search. Create lets you pick a sample photo, add a caption and post it to the top of the feed. Activity lists likes, comments and new followers. Profile shows post, follower and following counts, a bio and a 3-column grid. Add direct messages with a chat list and threads. Use realistic sample data and real Unsplash photos, and make every button work. No login yet.
```

It built Pinhole, with its own pinhole-camera logo, warm orange colors and all five tabs. The agent tested each tab in the simulator and fixed what it found, such as a cut-off logo.

## Publish it

Click **Publish**, then **Publish to App Store**, and Vibecode builds, signs, uploads and submits your Swift app for review. You need the Plus plan or higher and an [Apple Developer account](/guides/apple-developer-account), which costs $99 a year.

Before you submit a social app, add ways to report posts, block users and filter objectionable content, and list contact details. [Apple's guideline 1.2](https://developer.apple.com/app-store/review/guidelines/#user-generated-content) requires all four. Once people can sign up, they also need a way to delete their account in the app. [How to create a social media app](https://www.vibecodeapp.com/blog/how-to-make-a-social-media-app) has the one prompt we sent to add all of these to a real social app. [Publish to the App Store](/getting-started/deploy-app-store) has every step, and the [review guidelines checklist](/guides/app-store-review-guidelines) covers common rejections.

## Frequently asked questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="What is an Instagram clone?">
    An app with Instagram's core features under its own name and design. People build one to learn how social apps work, or to start a photo app for their own community.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Is there a free Instagram clone app?">
    Yes, with limits. Pixgram is free to copy, but running and changing it use credits. For a free start, sign up with Google or Apple and build from the [prompt above](#prompt-example) as your first project.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Is it legal to make an Instagram clone?">
    Yes, if it's your own brand. A photo feed and stories are common features, but Instagram's name, logo and other brand assets aren't yours to use, so Pixgram has its own name and logo. The [guide's FAQ](https://www.vibecodeapp.com/clones/instagram-clone#faq) has more.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can I publish an Instagram clone to the App Store?">
    Yes, if it has its own name, design and purpose, like a photo app for a running club. [Apple's guideline 4.1](https://developer.apple.com/app-store/review/guidelines/#copycats) rejects straight copies. See [Publish it](#publish-it) for the steps.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Does it work on Android?">
    Pixgram is native Swift, so it runs on iPhone and iPad. For Android, click **New app**, open **Legacy**, choose **Mobile app (Legacy)** and send the prompt above with "native iPhone" changed to "mobile". That builds a React Native app, and on the Pro and Max plans you can [download its code](/getting-started/download-code) and publish it on Google Play yourself. [Swift vs React Native](/guides/swift-vs-react-native) explains the difference.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Is there another app similar to Instagram?">
    Yes. [VSCO](https://www.vsco.co) pairs photo editing with a community, [Pixelfed](https://pixelfed.org) is a free, open-source photo-sharing network, and [Glass](https://glass.photo) is a paid community for photographers with no ads. Or build one for your own group from Pixgram.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
