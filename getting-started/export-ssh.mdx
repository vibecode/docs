<Note>SSH is paused. You can't connect to Vibecode projects over SSH right now. To work on your project from Cursor, Claude Code, or another AI tool, connect the [Vibecode MCP](/guides/vibecode-mcp) instead.</Note>

## Work on Your Project From Your Own Tools

Connect the [Vibecode MCP](/guides/vibecode-mcp) to Cursor, Claude Code, Codex, or another AI tool that supports MCP. Once connected, your AI tool can build and change apps, check build progress and logs, and, in coding tools, read your app's code and run commands. Connecting is free, and builds and changes use your Vibecode credits. The [Cursor guide](/guides/cursor) has the Cursor steps, and [vibecodeapp.com/mcp](https://www.vibecodeapp.com/mcp) has the server details.

## Export Your Code

You can still [download your code](/getting-started/download-code) from the web app on the Pro and Max plans.
