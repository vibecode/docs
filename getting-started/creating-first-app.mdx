**Vibecode is an AI app builder: you describe an app in plain English and its agent builds it as a native Swift and SwiftUI app for iPhone and iPad, a React Native app for iPhone and Android, or a web app.** You watch it come together in a live preview, and when it's ready you publish it to the Apple App Store from the Vibecode web app with your own Apple Developer account. Your first app build on a new account is free.

## What Vibecode Builds

| Project type | Language | Runs on | Publish to |
|---|---|---|---|
| **iOS app** | [Swift and SwiftUI](/getting-started/native-ios-apps) (native, not React Native) | iPhone and iPad | Apple App Store, or install on your own iPhone |
| **Mobile app** | React Native and Expo (TypeScript) | iPhone and Android | Apple App Store (via Expo Launch), or share a link |
| **Web app** | TypeScript | Any browser | A `yourapp.vibecode.run` URL or your own domain |

You don't need a Mac, Xcode, or coding experience. Here's how to build your first one.

## Step 1: Sign Up and Create a Project

1. Download the Vibecode app on [iOS](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/vibecode-ai-app-builder/id6742912146) or [Android](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.vibecodeapp.app), or open the [web app](https://vibecodeapp.com)
2. Create an account with your email
3. Tap **New project** in the iPhone app (or click **New app** on the web) and pick what you want to build:
   - **iOS app**: a [native Swift app](/getting-started/native-ios-apps) for iPhone and iPad
   - **Mobile app**: a React Native app for iOS and Android
   - **Web app**: a website or web app

   Not sure between iOS app and Mobile app? See [Swift vs React Native](/guides/swift-vs-react-native), or follow [How to Build an iPhone App with AI](/guides/build-iphone-app-with-ai) for the full walkthrough.

## Step 2: Describe Your App

Type a prompt that describes what you want your app to do. Be as detailed or as simple as you like — the AI will figure out the rest.

**Example prompts:**

> "Create a to-do list app where I can add tasks, mark them done, and swipe to delete"

> "Build a recipe tracker where I can save recipes with photos and search by ingredient"

> "Make a habit tracker with streaks and daily reminders"

<Tip>Start simple. You can always add more features later by chatting with the AI.</Tip>

## Step 3: Generate and Test

1. Send your prompt. Claude Code builds your app in real time, then [tests it on its own](/features/agent-testing) and fixes what it finds
2. Test it in the built-in preview. Native iOS apps run in a cloud iOS Simulator streamed to your screen; Mobile apps can also run on your phone by scanning the QR code
3. Tell the AI what to change: "Make the header blue" or "Add a settings page"

## Tips for Better Results

- **Go one feature at a time** — build the core, test it, then add more
- **Be specific when it matters** — "a Duolingo-style progress bar" beats "a progress bar"
- **Use the [Pinch to Build menu](/getting-started/pinch-to-build)** to quickly customize colors, layouts, and components
- **Attach screenshots or mockups** if you have a specific design in mind

## Frequently Asked Questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="What can I build with Vibecode?">
    Three kinds of apps: native Swift and SwiftUI apps for iPhone and iPad (**iOS app**), React Native apps that run on iPhone and Android (**Mobile app**), and websites or web apps (**Web app**). You pick the type when you create a project.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Is Vibecode only for React Native apps?">
    No. Vibecode builds native Swift and SwiftUI iPhone apps, React Native apps for iOS and Android, and web apps. Native iOS projects are standard Xcode projects with no React Native or web view. See [Native iOS Apps](/getting-started/native-ios-apps).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can Vibecode publish my app to the App Store?">
    Yes. On the web, click **Publish → Publish to App Store** (iOS apps) or **Deploy → Submit to App Store** (Mobile apps). In the Vibecode iPhone app, tap **Publish**. Vibecode builds and uploads the app with your Apple Developer account ($99/year from Apple). See [How to Publish an App to the App Store](/getting-started/deploy-app-store).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Is Vibecode free?">
    Your first app build on a new account is free. After that you need credits, which come with paid plans starting at $20/month, or with auto-reload. App Store submission is included from the Plus plan. See [Plans and Credits](/getting-started/plans-and-credits) and [pricing](https://www.vibecodeapp.com/pricing).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Do I need a Mac or coding experience?">
    No. Native iOS apps are built on cloud Macs and previewed in a cloud iOS Simulator streamed to your browser or phone. You build by chatting with the agent, and on the Pro and Max plans you can still [download the code](/getting-started/download-code) if you want it.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Which AI builds my app?">
    Vibecode runs [Claude Code](/getting-started/claude-code), Anthropic's coding agent, in a cloud sandbox for each project. Claude Opus 5.5 is the default model for new apps, and you can switch models in the model picker.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
