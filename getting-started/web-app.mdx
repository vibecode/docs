The Vibecode web app at [vibecodeapp.com](https://vibecodeapp.com) lets you build from any browser. You describe your idea, the agent builds it, and you test it in a live preview next to the chat.

It builds all three project types:

- **iOS app**: a native Swift app for iPhone and iPad. See [Native iOS Apps](/getting-started/native-ios-apps).
- **Mobile app**: a React Native app for iOS and Android
- **Web app**: a website or web app that runs in any browser

## Start a Project

- **From the home page:** type your idea in the prompt box ("What do you want to create?"). Use the platform picker in the prompt box to choose **Web**, **Mobile**, or **iOS**, then send.
- **From your projects:** click **New app** in the sidebar (or **Create** on the **Workspaces** page). Choose **Mobile app**, **Web app**, or **iOS app**, then describe what you want to build.

New to prompting? See [Creating Your First App](/getting-started/creating-first-app).

## Find Your Way Around

A project opens with the chat on the left and your app on the right.

### Prompt Box

- The model picker shows the current model. Click it to choose which AI model builds your app. Some models also let you set **Reasoning**.
- The camera button attaches a screenshot of the preview
- The mic button lets you dictate your prompt
- Drag files onto the prompt box to attach them

<Frame caption="The model picker in the prompt box">
  <img src="/images/screens/model-picker.png" alt="The model picker in the prompt box" style={{ maxWidth: "275px" }} />
</Frame>

### Preview

What you see depends on the project type:

- **Web app**: a browser preview. Switch between **Desktop**, **Tablet**, and **Mobile** sizes, or open it in a new tab.
- **Mobile app**: runs in the browser. Click **Open on mobile** for a QR code to preview it on your phone. You can also switch the simulator type to **iOS (beta)**.
- **iOS app**: a live stream of an iOS Simulator in the cloud. See [How the Preview Works](/getting-started/native-ios-apps#how-the-preview-works).

Use the tab bar to switch between **Preview** and **Code**, or open tools like **API**, **Env Var**, **Database**, and **Logs**. The **API** tab is where you add [integrations](/integrations/index).

### Top Bar

- Your credit balance, shown in dollars. Click it to buy more credits, turn on auto reload, or redeem a promo code. See [Plans and Credits](/getting-started/plans-and-credits).
- **`<>`**: code options, including [Download Code](/getting-started/download-code) on Pro and Max plans
- **Share**: once your app is published, copy its link or share it (Mobile and Web apps). Before that, it asks you to publish first.
- **Deploy** or **Publish**: see [Publish Your App](#publish-your-app)

<Frame caption="Click your credit balance to buy credits, compare plans, or set up auto reload">

The menu next to your project name has **Change Icon**, **Duplicate**, **Hard Restart**, **Export chat**, and **Delete**.

Your projects are listed on the **Workspaces** page. **Settings** is in the sidebar there.

## Publish Your App

The button in the top right depends on the project type:

| Project type | Button | What's inside |
|---|---|---|
| **iOS app** | **Publish** | **Install** on your iPhone, or **Publish to App Store** |
| **Mobile app** | **Deploy** | **Submit to App Store**, and deploy your backend if your app has one |
| **Web app** | **Deploy** | Put your app online at a live URL |

<Frame caption="Publish menu for an iOS app">
  <img src="/images/screens/publish-menu-ios.png" alt="Publish menu for an iOS app" style={{ maxWidth: "500px" }} />
</Frame>

Once your app has been deployed, **Deploy** changes to **Deployments**.

Not sure which path fits? See [Choose Your Deployment Path](/deploying/overview).

## Keep Working While the Agent Builds

You don't have to wait for the agent to finish.

- **Queue prompts.** While the agent works, the prompt box shows "Queue more prompts". Type your next request and click **Queue**.
- **Manage the queue.** Queued prompts sit above the prompt box (for example, "2 in queue"). Click to open the list, then hover a prompt to edit or delete it.
- **Stop** ends the current run and clears the queue.

Queued prompts run in order, one at a time, as each run finishes. They're saved with your project, so they still run if you refresh or close the tab.

<Tip>After you send a prompt, Vibecode suggests keeping the tab open in the background and offers **Enable notifications**. Turn them on and your browser tells you when your app is ready. On desktop this shows as a pop-up message; in a phone browser, as a card above the prompt box.</Tip>

On Web and Mobile apps, the agent may test your app itself. When it does, the preview shows **Agent is testing your app** and you can watch it work.
