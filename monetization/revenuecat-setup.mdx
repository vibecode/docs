**To sell subscriptions or in-app purchases in an iPhone app, you use Apple's in-app purchase system (App Review Guideline 3.1.1 requires it for digital goods; US App Store apps may also link to a web checkout), create the products in App Store Connect, and show them on a paywall with clear prices and a Restore Purchases button.** Vibecode does most of this with [RevenueCat](https://www.revenuecat.com/), which handles purchases, receipts, and subscription status for you: connect RevenueCat in your project's **Payment** tab, ask the agent to create your products and paywall, sync the products to App Store Connect, then test with a sandbox account. RevenueCat is free up to $2,500 in monthly tracked revenue, then 1% of tracked revenue.

<Info>
  The RevenueCat **Payment** tab is for **Mobile app** (React Native) projects. It isn't available for native Swift **iOS app** projects yet. Charging on the web? Use [Stripe](/monetization/stripe-setup) instead.
</Info>

## In-App Purchases at a Glance

| | What it is | Where |
|---|---|---|
| **Apple Developer Program** | Required to sell anything on the App Store | $99/year ([enroll](/guides/apple-developer-account)) |
| **Paid Apps Agreement, tax, and banking** | Must be active before any purchase works, even in testing | App Store Connect → **Business** |
| **RevenueCat account** | Handles purchases, receipts, and subscriber status | Free up to $2,500 monthly tracked revenue, then 1% ([pricing](https://www.revenuecat.com/pricing/)) |
| **Products** | Your subscriptions and one-time purchases | Created by the agent in RevenueCat, synced to App Store Connect |
| **Paywall** | The screen that shows your offers | Built by the agent in your app |
| **Apple's commission** | Apple's share of each sale | 15% in the App Store Small Business Program (up to $1 million a year in proceeds), the standard rate above that |

## Step 3: Configure RevenueCat Credentials

<Note>This step is critical! Without these credentials, your in-app purchases will not work.</Note>

For RevenueCat to communicate with App Store Connect, you need to set up two API keys in your RevenueCat dashboard:

### App Store Connect API Key

1. Go to [App Store Connect](https://appstoreconnect.apple.com/) → **Users and Access** → **Integrations** → **App Store Connect API**
2. Click **Generate API Key** with "App Manager" access level
3. Download the `.p8` file (you can only download this once!)
4. Note your **Issuer ID** from this page
5. In RevenueCat, go to your app's settings and upload the `.p8` file
6. Enter your Issuer ID and Vendor number (found in App Store Connect under **Payments and Financial Reports**)

<Card title="App Store Connect API Key Configuration" icon="book" href="https://www.revenuecat.com/docs/service-credentials/itunesconnect-app-specific-shared-secret/app-store-connect-api-key-configuration">
  Full Guide
</Card>

### In-App Purchase Key

1. Go to [App Store Connect](https://appstoreconnect.apple.com/) → **Users and Access** → **Integrations** → **In-App Purchase**
2. Click **Generate In-App Purchase Key**
3. Download the `.p8` file (you can only download this once!)
4. In RevenueCat, upload this key in the **In-app purchase key configuration** section
5. Add your Issuer ID and save

<Card title="In-App Purchase Key Configuration" icon="book" href="https://www.revenuecat.com/docs/service-credentials/itunesconnect-app-specific-shared-secret/in-app-purchase-key-configuration">
  In-App Purchase Key Configuration
</Card>

<Warning>The In-App Purchase key is required. Without it, transactions will fail to be recorded when using StoreKit 2.</Warning>

## Step 8: Complete Product Setup in App Store Connect

Your product was created but needs additional metadata before it can go live:

1. Go to [App Store Connect](https://appstoreconnect.apple.com/)
2. Click on your app in the bottom left
3. Navigate to **Monetization** → **Subscriptions** (or **In-App Purchases**)
4. Click on the subscription group that was created
5. Select your product

## Apple's Rules for In-App Purchases and Paywalls

Apple checks these during App Review. Getting them wrong is a common reason for rejection under guideline 3.1:

- **Use Apple's in-app purchase for digital goods.** Subscriptions, premium features, in-game currency, and content used inside the app go through Apple (3.1.1). On the US App Store you may also link to your own web checkout; elsewhere that needs an Apple entitlement (3.1.1(a)). Physical goods and real-world services, such as deliveries or rides, use other payment methods.
- **Show clear prices and terms.** The paywall must say what the user gets, how much it costs, and how often they're billed.
- **Include Restore Purchases.** Users who reinstall or switch devices must be able to get back what they paid for.
- **Subscriptions must last at least 7 days** and provide ongoing value (3.1.2(a)).
- **Link your terms of use and privacy policy** on the paywall and in your App Store listing.
- **Submit your products with the app.** New in-app purchases must have complete metadata in App Store Connect and be submitted for review together with an app version.

In Mobile app projects, the App Store [Preflight](/getting-started/deploy-app-store#run-preflight-first) check reviews your paywall for Restore Purchases, clear pricing, and non-Apple payment code before you submit. More in the [App Store rejections checklist](/guides/app-store-review-guidelines).

## Troubleshooting

There are many common issues that can prevent your products from working correctly. Please go through all of them and make sure they do not impact you.

**Can't connect to RevenueCat?**
- Check that popups aren't blocked in your browser
- Try refreshing the page and connecting again
- Ensure you're signed into your RevenueCat account

**Products not showing up?**
- Click **Refresh** in the Payment Dashboard
- Verify products are created in both App Store Connect and RevenueCat
- Check that products are linked to the correct app in RevenueCat

**Product shows "Missing Metadata"?**
- Go to App Store Connect and add all required fields
- Make sure to upload a screenshot for the product
- Save your changes and the status will update

**Purchases failing in testing?**
- Ensure you're using a valid Sandbox Tester account
- Sign out of your regular Apple ID first
- Check that your app's bundle ID matches App Store Connect

**Products not appearing in TestFlight?**

When subscriptions or in-app purchase products don't appear in TestFlight, it's usually due to an incomplete setup in App Store Connect—missing legal entity information and incomplete agreements or tax information.

<img src="/payments/asc-legal.png" alt="App Store Connect Business Agreements" style={{maxHeight: '400px', display: 'block', margin: '0 auto'}} className="rounded-lg" />

To fix this:
- Go to [App Store Connect](https://appstoreconnect.apple.com/) → **Business** → **Agreements**
- Ensure your **Paid Apps Agreement** is active (not "New")
- Complete any pending legal entity information
- Fill out all required tax and banking information
- Make sure to also fill out all the metadata for your products in App Store Connect. You can do this by going to App -> Subscriptions -> Your Subscription -> Edit -> Metadata.

**Pending agreements blocking purchases?**

If you see a yellow warning banner in App Store Connect about updated agreements, you must clear all pending agreements before RevenueCat can process purchases.

<img src="/payments/asc-agreements.png" alt="App Store Connect pending agreements warning" style={{maxHeight: '400px', display: 'block', margin: '0 auto'}} className="rounded-lg" />

To fix this:
- The **Account Holder** (not just any team member) must sign in to App Store Connect
- Review and accept any updated license agreements shown in the yellow banner
- Check **Business** → **Agreements** for any other pending agreements
- Once all agreements are accepted, the yellow warning will disappear and purchases will work

## Frequently Asked Questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Do I have to use Apple's in-app purchase?">
    For digital content, features, and subscriptions used in your iPhone app, in-app purchase is required ([guideline 3.1.1](https://developer.apple.com/app-store/review/guidelines/)). On the US App Store you can also add a button or link to your own web checkout; outside the US that needs an Apple entitlement, which only some countries offer (3.1.1(a)). RevenueCat runs on top of Apple's in-app purchase system. Physical goods and services used outside the app can use other payment methods.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="How much does RevenueCat cost?">
    RevenueCat is free until your app makes $2,500 in monthly tracked revenue. Above that it charges 1% of tracked revenue. Apple's commission is separate. Check [RevenueCat's pricing page](https://www.revenuecat.com/pricing/) for current terms.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="How much does Apple take from in-app purchases?">
    15% if you're in the App Store Small Business Program (up to $1 million in proceeds a year), and the standard commission above that. Apple's rates differ by country and program, so check App Store Connect for yours.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can I test purchases without being charged?">
    Yes. Purchases in TestFlight and development builds use Apple's sandbox, so nobody is charged. For development builds, create a sandbox tester in App Store Connect under **Users and Access → Sandbox** and sign in with it on your test device.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Why aren't my products showing up?">
    Usually one of three things: the **Paid Apps Agreement** isn't active, the product is missing metadata (including its review screenshot) in App Store Connect, or the bundle ID doesn't match. See [Troubleshooting](#troubleshooting) above.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can I add in-app purchases to a native Swift iOS app?">
    The guided RevenueCat setup in the **Payment** tab isn't available for **iOS app** (Swift) projects yet. It's built for **Mobile app** (React Native) projects. See [Swift vs React Native](/guides/swift-vs-react-native).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can I use Stripe instead?">
    For web apps, yes: see [Stripe Setup](/monetization/stripe-setup). Inside an iPhone app, digital goods use Apple's in-app purchase, and on the US App Store you can also link to a Stripe checkout on your website (3.1.1(a)).
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
