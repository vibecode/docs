**Charge for a mobile app with Apple's in-app purchase system, which Vibecode sets up through [RevenueCat](/monetization/revenuecat-setup), and charge on the web with [Stripe](/monetization/stripe-setup).** Apple requires in-app purchase for digital content, features, and subscriptions sold inside an iPhone app ([guideline 3.1.1](https://developer.apple.com/app-store/review/guidelines/)). Apps on the US App Store may also link to a web checkout such as Stripe; in other countries that needs an Apple entitlement, offered only in some of them (3.1.1(a)). Physical goods and real-world services always use other payment methods.

## Which One to Use

| Your project | What you're selling | Use |
|---|---|---|
| **Mobile app** (React Native) | Subscriptions, premium features, one-time unlocks | [RevenueCat](/monetization/revenuecat-setup) in the **Payment** tab |
| **Web app** | Subscriptions, one-time payments, checkout | [Stripe](/monetization/stripe-setup) in the **Payment** tab |
| **iOS app** (native Swift) | Subscriptions and in-app purchases | The guided **Payment** tab setup isn't available for iOS app projects yet |

## Use RevenueCat If

- you're charging inside a mobile app
- you need App Store subscriptions or in-app purchases
- you want the agent to create your products and paywall and sync them to App Store Connect

## Use Stripe If

- you're charging on the web
- you need checkout or subscriptions for a web app

Before you submit a paid app, check Apple's payment rules in the [App Store rejections checklist](/guides/app-store-review-guidelines).
