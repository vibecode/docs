**To use your own domain for a Vibecode web app, open your deployment's Domain tab, click Add Custom Domain, enter the domain, add the CNAME (or ALIAS) records Vibecode shows you at your domain provider, click Check now to verify, then deploy.** Your app is then served at your domain over HTTPS instead of the default `yourapp.vibecode.run` address. You need a deployed web app and a domain you own, bought from any registrar such as Cloudflare, GoDaddy, or Namecheap.

*Last updated: October 10, 2026*

## Custom Domain Setup at a Glance

| Step | Where | What you do |
|---|---|---|
| 1. Deploy your web app | Vibecode | [Deploy it](/deploying/guide) so it has a `vibecode.run` address |
| 2. Add the domain | Vibecode → **Domain** tab | **Add Custom Domain** → **I have a domain** → enter `example.com` or `app.example.com` |
| 3. Add DNS records | Your domain provider | A CNAME (or ALIAS/ANAME for a root domain) pointing to `<your-project>.vibecode.run` |
| 4. Verify | Vibecode → **Domain** tab | Click **Check now** once DNS has updated |
| 5. Deploy | Vibecode | Deploy to activate the domain |

<Tip>Just want a nicer free address? You can change your app's `vibecode.run` subdomain in the **Domain** tab without buying a domain.</Tip>

## Before You Start

You'll need:

- A **deployed Vibecode web app** (see [Deploying Your Web App](/deploying/guide) if you haven't deployed yet)
- A **domain you own** with access to its DNS settings (e.g. via Cloudflare, GoDaddy, Namecheap, etc.)

## Step 1: Open the Domain Tab

In your deployment dashboard, click the **Domain** tab.

## Step 2: Add Your Domain

Click the **Add Custom Domain** button. You'll see a modal with two options.

<img
  src="/deploying/custom-domain/02-add-domain-modal.png"
  alt="Add Custom Domain modal with 'I have a domain' and 'Maybe later' options"
  style={{ maxHeight: "400px", display: "block", margin: "0 auto" }}
  className="rounded-lg"
/>

Click **"I have a domain"** to continue.

## Step 3: Enter Your Domain

Type in your domain name, just the domain, with no `http://` or `https://` prefix.

<img
  src="/deploying/custom-domain/03-enter-domain.png"
  alt="Domain input field with www redirect checkbox"
  style={{ maxHeight: "400px", display: "block", margin: "0 auto" }}
  className="rounded-lg"
/>

- Enter your domain (e.g. `example.com`)
- Optionally check **"Redirect www to this domain"** if you want `www.example.com` to redirect to your apex domain
- Click **Save**

## Step 4: Configure DNS Records

After saving, Vibecode shows you the DNS records you need to add at your domain provider. The exact records depend on whether you're connecting an **apex domain** or a **subdomain**.

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Apex domain (e.g. example.com)">
    <img
      src="/deploying/custom-domain/04b-dns-records-apex.png"
      alt="DNS records for apex domain showing ALIAS and CNAME records"
      style={{ maxHeight: "500px", display: "block", margin: "0 auto" }}
      className="rounded-lg"
    />

    | Type | Name | Target |
    |------|------|--------|
    | CNAME or ALIAS | `@` | `<your-project>.vibecode.run` |
    | CNAME | `www` | `<your-project>.vibecode.run` |
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Subdomain (e.g. app.example.com)">
    <img
      src="/deploying/custom-domain/04-dns-records.png"
      alt="DNS records for subdomain showing CNAME records"
      style={{ maxHeight: "500px", display: "block", margin: "0 auto" }}
      className="rounded-lg"
    />

    | Type | Name | Target |
    |------|------|--------|
    | CNAME | `app` | `<your-project>.vibecode.run` |
    | CNAME | `www.app` | `<your-project>.vibecode.run` |
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

<Note>
  The `www` record only appears if you enabled the www redirect in the previous step.
</Note>

<Tip>
  Some DNS providers don't support CNAME records on the apex domain (`@`). In that case, use an **ALIAS** or **ANAME** record instead.
</Tip>

## Step 6: Verify Your Domain

Once you've added the DNS records, go back to the Domain tab in Vibecode and click **Check now**.

<Note>
  DNS changes can take a while to propagate. If verification fails, wait a bit and try again.
</Note>

Once verified, you'll see a confirmation banner showing the domain is routed correctly. Click **Done** to finish, then deploy to activate your custom domain.

## Troubleshooting

<img
  src="/deploying/custom-domain/05-troubleshoot.png"
  alt="Troubleshoot section showing Cloudflare proxy tip"
  style={{ maxHeight: "500px", display: "block", margin: "0 auto" }}
  className="rounded-lg"
/>

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Cloudflare proxy conflict">
    If you're using Cloudflare, make sure the DNS record is set to **DNS-only mode** rather than **Proxied**.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="A or AAAA record conflict">
    If you already have A or AAAA records pointing your domain somewhere else, they can conflict with the CNAME record. Remove the old records first.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="DNS propagation delay">
    DNS changes do not take effect instantly. Wait a bit and try the verification again.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Need to start over?">
    You can click **Remove domain** in the Domain tab to disconnect your custom domain and start over.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Frequently Asked Questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Can I use a custom domain with a free vibecode.run address?">
    Every deployed web app gets a free `yourapp.vibecode.run` address, and you can change the subdomain in the **Domain** tab. A custom domain like `example.com` has to be bought from a domain registrar.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Should I use example.com or www.example.com?">
    Either works. Enter the one you want people to see, and check **Redirect www to this domain** if you enter the root domain so `www.example.com` sends visitors there too.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="How long does it take for a custom domain to work?">
    Often minutes, but DNS changes can take longer to spread depending on your provider. If **Check now** fails, wait and try again.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Do I need to set up SSL or HTTPS?">
    No separate certificate setup is needed in Vibecode. Once the domain is verified and deployed, open it at `https://` your domain.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Why won't my domain verify on Cloudflare?">
    Set the record to **DNS only** (grey cloud) instead of **Proxied**, and remove any old A or AAAA records for the same name.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can I connect a custom domain to an iPhone app?">
    Custom domains are for web apps and backends served at a URL. iPhone apps are installed from the App Store. See [How to Publish an App to the App Store](/getting-started/deploy-app-store).
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
