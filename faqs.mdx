## General

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="What is Vibecode?">
    Vibecode is the easiest way to build apps using natural language. Available on iOS, Android, and the web at vibecodeapp.com, you can generate fully functional apps with prompting, test them in real-time, and share with anyone instantly.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What makes Vibecode different from other AI app builders?">
    Vibecode is unique because it:
    - Allows you to build and test directly on your mobile device
    - Supports one-click sharing via published links without requiring App Store submission
    - Builds [native Swift iOS apps](/getting-started/native-ios-apps), cross-platform React Native apps, and web apps
    - Provides real-time preview and iteration capabilities
    - Offers extensive [integrations](/integrations/index) with popular APIs and services
    - Gives you full access to [export your source code](/getting-started/download-code)
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="How do I share my apps?">
    You can share your apps in multiple ways:
    - **[Sharing](/getting-started/sharing-app)**: Share instantly with anyone via a published link — no App Store submission required
    - **[Export Code](/getting-started/download-code)**: Download your complete source code and deploy it yourself
    - **[App Store](/getting-started/deploy-app-store)**: Submit your app to the Apple App Store for public distribution

    Sharing by published link is the fastest way to let people try your app immediately.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What can I build with Vibecode?">
    Native Swift apps for iPhone and iPad, cross-platform React Native apps for iOS and Android, and web apps. The world is your oyster — you can build whatever comes to your mind. Check out the [Example Projects](/getting-started/use-case-1) for inspiration.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Do I need coding experience to build with Vibecode?">
    No coding experience is required! Vibecode is designed for everyone, from complete beginners to experienced developers. You simply describe what you want in natural language, and the AI handles the technical implementation. If you do have coding experience, you can [download your code](/getting-started/download-code) or [connect via SSH](/getting-started/export-ssh) for deeper customization.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Is there a Vibecode Android app?">
    Yes. The Vibecode Android app is available on [Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.vibecodeapp.app). Android runs Mobile app (React Native) projects; native iOS projects are Swift apps and run on iPhone.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can I use Vibecode to build production-ready apps?">
    Yes! Vibecode generates production-quality code that you can use for real applications. Many users have successfully built and deployed production apps using Vibecode. You have full control over the generated code and can export it for further customization or deployment.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can I use Vibecode for professional purposes? E.g. for my work or my business?">
    Absolutely! Vibecode is suitable for both personal and professional use. Many businesses and professionals use Vibecode to:
    - Prototype ideas quickly
    - Build internal tools and apps
    - Create client projects
    - Develop commercial applications

    You own the code you generate and can use it however you like.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Will Vibecoded apps work on Android and iOS?">
    It depends on the platform you pick when you create a project:
    - **iOS app**: a [native Swift and SwiftUI app](/getting-started/native-ios-apps) for iPhone and iPad. No React Native. It runs on Apple devices only.
    - **Mobile app**: a React Native and Expo app. One codebase runs on both iOS and Android.
    - **Web app**: runs in any browser.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="How long does it take to build an app with Vibecode?">
    It depends on the complexity of your app. Simple apps can be built in minutes, while more complex apps with multiple features might take longer. The beauty of Vibecode is that you can iterate and refine quickly using natural language.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Building Tips & Best Practices

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="How do I build efficiently with Vibecode?">
    Start by simplifying your idea into the essential features, then build from there. It also helps to have an initial prompt that details the expected outcome in terms of the look, feel, and functionality. See our [Prompt Templates](/getting-started/web-to-mobile) for examples.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="How should I structure my prompts for the best results?">
    Our AI Agent is pretty good at coming up with what is needed for building your app regardless of the prompt, but for specific features it is best to be as specific as possible. You can even suggest examples (e.g., "An app that is designed like Duolingo" or "make the app have the Google color scheme"). Check out our [Native UI Components](/getting-started/native-ui-components) page for copy-paste prompts.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What should I do when I hit an error?">
    Either copy and paste the error back into the chat, or tell the AI agent that you got an error and it should try to resolve it. See our [Troubleshooting guide](/troubleshooting) for common issues.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Features

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="How do I add payments or paywalls to my app?">
    You can integrate payment systems into your Vibecode app. Popular options include:

    **[RevenueCat](/monetization/revenuecat-setup)**: For in-app purchases and subscriptions — ask the AI to "integrate RevenueCat for subscriptions" and add your API keys via the ENV tab.

    **[Stripe](/monetization/stripe-setup)**: For one-time payments and custom payment flows — prompt the AI to "add Stripe payment integration" and configure via the ENV tab.

    **Important**: Never hardcode API keys in your app. Always use environment variables via the Vibecode ENV tab.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="How do I publish my app to the App Store?">
    Vibecode has a built-in App Store submission flow — no need to use Xcode manually.

    1. Open your project and tap the app name (mobile) or click Share (web)
    2. Select **Publish on App Store** / **Deploy to App Store**
    3. Follow the step-by-step wizard to configure your app, add your Expo token, and start Expo Launch

    You'll need an [Apple Developer Program membership](https://developer.apple.com/programs/) ($99/year) and an [Expo account](https://expo.dev/signup) (free) for Mobile app projects.

    Native iOS app projects skip Expo: click **Publish**, choose **Publish to App Store**, and connect an App Store Connect API key. Vibecode builds, signs, and uploads your Swift app to Apple for you. See [Native iOS Apps](/getting-started/native-ios-apps#publish-to-the-app-store).

    See the full guide: [Deploy to App Store](/getting-started/deploy-app-store)
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="How do I add a backend, database, or authentication to my app?">
    **[Vibecode Cloud](/features/vibecode-cloud)** is the easiest way — it gives you a database, authentication, and file storage with a single prompt. Just tell the AI "add a database and authentication to my app" and it sets everything up automatically.

    Vibecode Cloud is available on Plus plans and above. See the [Backend & Auth guide](/features/backend-auth) for details on how it works under the hood.

    You can also integrate external services like Supabase or Firebase if you prefer — just prompt the AI to add the integration.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="How do I start a new chat with the agent?">
    Look for the "New Chat" or "+" button in the Vibecode workspace. This creates a fresh conversation while preserving your existing chats. You can always return to previous chats by selecting them from your chat history.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="How can I download my project's code?">
    Open your project in the web app, click the **`<>`** button, and select "Download Code." Check your email for the download link. You get the complete project: an Xcode project for iOS apps, a React Native/Expo project for mobile apps, or a web project. Run it locally, modify it, and deploy it.

    See the full guide: [Download Your Code](/getting-started/download-code)
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Data & Integrations

    Yes! [Vibecode Cloud](/features/vibecode-cloud) provides a built-in backend for your app with database, authentication, and file storage. Just tell the AI "add a database and authentication to my app" and it sets everything up automatically.

    Vibecode Cloud is available on Plus plans and above. See the [Vibecode Cloud guide](/features/vibecode-cloud) for details.

    You can also integrate with external services like Supabase or Firebase if you prefer.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can I add login to my app?">
    Yes! With [Vibecode Cloud](/features/vibecode-cloud) (available on Plus plans and above), you can add user authentication to your app. Tell the AI "add login to my app" and it will set up Better Auth with Vibecode's default email OTP flow.

    For Google Sign-In, see the [Google Sign-In setup guide](/google-login/setup).
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Technical

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="How do I connect to my app using SSH?">
    Navigate to the Export/SSH section in the [Pinch to Build menu](/getting-started/pinch-to-build), or use the project dropdown. Click "Connect via SSH" and follow the instructions sent to your email.

    See the full guide: [Export and SSH](/getting-started/export-ssh)
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Are Vibecode apps actually native iOS/Android apps? Or is it just web apps?">
    They are real apps, not web apps. Choose **iOS app** and Vibecode writes a [native Swift and SwiftUI app](/getting-started/native-ios-apps), compiled with Xcode, with no React Native or web view in between. Choose **Mobile app** for a React Native and Expo app that runs on both iOS and Android.

    Both have full access to native device features (camera, sensors, notifications) and can be published to the App Store.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Is Vibecode React Native or native Swift?">
    Both, and you choose per project. **iOS app** projects are native Swift and SwiftUI for iPhone and iPad. **Mobile app** projects are React Native and Expo, for when you need one codebase on iOS and Android. You pick the platform when you create the project.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="How do native iOS apps run without running code on my device?">
    While you build, your Swift app is compiled and runs in an iOS Simulator in the cloud. What you see in the browser or the Vibecode app is a live video stream of that simulator, and your taps are sent back to it. No app code runs on your phone.

    To put the app on your own iPhone, you need a paid Apple Developer Program membership. Click **Publish**, choose **Install**, and follow the steps:

    1. Connect an App Store Connect API key so Vibecode can sign with your Apple Developer account.
    2. Register your iPhone: scan the QR code with it, or tap **Register this iPhone** if you're already on it. The build can't start until a device is registered.
    3. Click **Build and install**, then open the install link on your iPhone.
    4. If iOS says the app needs Developer Mode, turn it on in **Settings → Privacy & Security → Developer Mode** and restart your iPhone.

    See [Native iOS Apps](/getting-started/native-ios-apps#install-on-your-iphone) for the full walkthrough.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can I use Vibecode to build a web app?">
    Yes! Vibecode now includes a website and web app builder. Simply prompt the AI to "create a website" or "create a web app" and it will generate the appropriate code. See [Building on Web](/getting-started/web-app) for details.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Account & Billing

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="How do I upgrade my account?">
    **Web:** Open a project, click your credit balance at the top, then click **Compare plans**.

    **iPhone:** Click the Profile or Settings button and scroll down to "Upgrade Subscription."

    See [Plans and Credits](/getting-started/plans-and-credits) for plan details and pricing.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Does Vibecode store or own my app data or code?">
    No, you own all the code and data you create with Vibecode. You retain full ownership, can export your code at any time, and can delete your projects whenever you want. We don't claim any intellectual property rights to your creations.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="How do I cancel my subscription?">
    **Web (Stripe):** Head to the [Stripe billing portal](https://billing.stripe.com/p/login/6oE8Ae7Xj8z8bPa8ww), sign in, and cancel your subscription there.

    **iOS:** Follow the [Apple cancellation instructions](https://support.apple.com/guide/iphone/view-or-cancel-subscriptions-iph4e3e7324f/ios).

    Your subscription remains active until the end of your billing period.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
